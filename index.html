About Me

I'm Gilbson — a Haitian-born IT professional with a passion for technology, fitness, and food. When I'm not troubleshooting systems or studying for my next AWS certification, you'll find me at the gym, experimenting in the kitchen, or deep in a movie marathon.

I believe the best technologists are well-rounded people. The discipline I've built in the gym, the creativity I bring to cooking, and the storytelling I appreciate in film all shape how I approach problem-solving and operations.
